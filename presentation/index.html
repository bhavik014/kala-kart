10 • Judge Q&A Defense

Bullet-Proof Answers to Tough Jury Questions

Q1: How do you handle heavy background noise (cows, rain, chatter) and rare village dialects? ▼

Answer: 1) Authenticity Asset: Subtle village ambience proves the product is genuine and hand-made rather than from a factory. 2) Bhashini NLP: Pre-trained on rural Indian acoustic models across 22 scheduled languages. 3) Fallback: If recognition confidence is low, the artisan taps one craft archetype button without typing.

Q2: How do you prevent counterfeit factory goods from claiming artisan GI status? ▼

Answer: 3-Tier Verification: 1) Artisans are authenticated via their Gram Panchayat verified PM Vishwakarma ID or SHG cluster number. 2) Computer Vision detects natural irregular brushwork and organic dye spectra instead of chemical CMYK print dots. 3) Unique SHA-256 cryptographic QR seal on physical postal packaging.

Q3: How will India Post pick up from remote hamlets without post offices? ▼

Answer: India Post has 150,000+ branch post offices covering 90%+ rural India. Checkout automatically generates a Speed Post barcode and notifies the local Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) on their official handheld DARPAN terminal for daily doorstep pickup.

Q4: Why not just use Amazon Karigar or Flipkart Samarth? ▼

Answer: Amazon Karigar requires GSTIN, trademark registration, English web forms, and 1000x1000 studio photos, charging up to 30% fees. Kala Kart is 100% voice-first in regional languages, requires zero typing, enhances village photos automatically, and legally guarantees 85%+ direct remittance.

Q5: How do illiterate artisans withdraw money without net banking? ▼

Answer: All funds settle to the artisan's Jan Dhan account. Artisans withdraw cash via Aadhaar-Enabled Payment System (AePS) using their fingerprint at their doorstep with the visiting postman (Micro-ATM).
