KALA KART (कला कार्ट)
❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
🎙️ Detailed Explanation
  • 10-Sec Voice Listing: Artisans speak in native vernacular dialects; system auto-extracts craft title, materials, story, and specs.
  • Authentic Voice Preservation: Genuine recorded artisan voice notes are permanently preserved and streamed via interactive buyer soundwave players.
  • CV AI Studio (5500K): Transforms poor village hut smartphone photos into studio-grade catalog shots with mineral dye calibration.
  • Fair Wage Pricing Engine: Computes fair retail prices using Ministry of Labour daily skilled wage benchmarks.
  • India Post Doorstep Pickup: Automated Speed Post booking directly from rural pin codes.
🎯 Addressing the Problem
  • Overcomes Digital Illiteracy: Zero typing or complex English menus; 100% voice-first interface in regional dialects (Bhojpuri, Maithili, etc.).
  • Eliminates Middlemen: Replaces exploitative aggregators taking up to 80% margins, returning 85%+ Direct Payout to artisans.
  • Solves Photo Rejections: Eliminates commercial photography constraints without expensive camera equipment or studios.
  • Transparent Remittance: Funds disburse straight into PM Vishwakarma linked DBT accounts.
✨ Innovation & Uniqueness
  • Human-Touch Audio Commerce: Urban buyers connect emotionally with the real artisan's voice and heritage story.
  • 6 Sovereign Pillars: Interoperable with PM Vishwakarma, GI Registry, Bhashini (MeitY), India Post, SFURTI, and Kala Sakhi.
  • Cryptographic GI Provenance: SHA-256 tamper-proof QR certificates authenticating regional craftsmanship.
  • Zero Paid SaaS Stacks: Built on open-source Python, FastAPI, SQLite, and serverless architectures.

⚙️ Technologies to be Used

  • Backend & Microservices: Python 3.10+, FastAPI (ASGI), Uvicorn, Mangum (AWS Lambda & Vercel Serverless Ready).

  • AI & Speech Models: Bhashini Open APIs (AI4Bharat IndicTrans2 & IndicConformer ASR/TTS) for 22 Indic dialects.
  • Computer Vision Studio: PIL/Pillow (5500K daylight white balancing, micro-contrast enhancement, natural dye swatches).
  • Logistics & KYC: India Post CEPT API bridge, QR Code generator, PM Vishwakarma e-KYC integration.
🔄 Methodology & Process
  • Audio Timeslicing (250ms): Continuous streaming audio capture with format transcoding (.webm, .ogg, .mp4).
  • Dialect Normalization: Real-time speech-to-text with bilingual entity extraction (craft type, colors, dimensions).
  • Photometric Image Correction: Mathematical luminance curves removing harsh village hut shadows without bleaching pigments.
  • Statutory Wage Formula: Price = (Days × Wage) + Materials + GI_Margin calibrated against Ministry benchmarks.
  • Status: 100% Working Prototype Live & Deployed.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
📊 Feasibility Analysis
  • Technical Feasibility: Ultra-lightweight footprint (<150ms API latency); runs smoothly on low-bandwidth 2G/3G mobile networks.
  • Economic Feasibility: Built entirely on open-source stacks with zero licensing or subscription fees, ensuring near-zero per-listing transaction costs.
  • Operational Feasibility: Leverages existing national grassroots infrastructure—India Post's 155,000+ branch post offices and CSC VLE networks.
  • Deployment Readiness: Dockerized, serverless-compatible microservices deployable on NIC Cloud / MeghRaj.
⚠️ Potential Challenges & Risks
  • Dialect & Acoustic Noise: Heavy rural background noise (cattle, machinery, wind) and non-standard colloquial dialects.
  • Poor Camera Optics: Motion blur, harsh shadows, and lens distortion from entry-level low-cost smartphones.
  • Digital Hesitation: Reluctance and fear of technology among elderly, illiterate generational artisans.
  • Internet Intermittency: Frequent cellular network dropouts in deep tribal belts.
🛡️ Mitigation Strategies
  • Dialect Noise Filtering: Client-side audio waveform validation paired with Bhashini IndicConformer noise-filtering pipelines.
  • Photometric Equalization: Dynamic CLAHE algorithms that auto-bracket exposure and correct chromatic distortions.
  • "Kala Sakhi" Model: Doorstep assisted onboarding by trained village women SHG leaders (VLEs) who visit homes.
  • Offline-First Caching: Local SQLite & IndexedDB storage queues orders and voice recordings when offline.
